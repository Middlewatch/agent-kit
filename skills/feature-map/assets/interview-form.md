# Feature map interview form

Copy this into the prompt and fill in what you can. Skip anything you do not have an
answer for; the agent will ask. Specific beats general: name the actual thing you reach
for ("magit's staging view"), not the category ("git integration").

## What this is

One or two sentences. Who uses it, what they get out of it.

## What it is not

The things you do not want this to become, in your own words. Products or features you
have seen go wrong. This becomes the non-goals list.

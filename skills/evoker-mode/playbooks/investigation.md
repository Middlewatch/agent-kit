### Investigation

**You own a cited answer, not a change.** For "how does X work", "why was Y
built this way", "are we sure about Z", "should we do X or Y". Read-only: a
fix discovered en route is reported, not made. Distinct from Prototype, which
builds a throwaway artifact when observation answers faster than reading.

1. Route the question: mechanism or placement → the `how` walkthrough (`~/.agents/kit/prompts/how.md`); history,
   rationale, or "where did this come from" → the `why` skill; a contested
   "are we sure" gets both.
2. Mark the read-only boundary: no product edits, scratch and probe scripts go
   to `$PI_SCRATCHPAD`.
3. Gather with citations. Delegate wide reads to explore children per the
   delegation standard; verify any claim you repeat against the source.
4. Answer from the evidence: `path:line`, commit, or URL for every specific;
   inference labeled as inference.
5. If the answer settled a durable question, file it (wiki note per
   `deep-research` finish, or the `adr` skill when it settled a decision).

**Reply:** the answer first, then the evidence trail, then confidence and what
would change it. Name anything surprising worth `harvest`.

# Research Workspace

This root (the research root named in `~/.agents/MACHINE.md`) is for evidence-backed research, synthesis, and writing. Research
happens here, and durable curated knowledge lives in `~/.agents/wiki`.

## Contract

- Prefer primary sources and current documentation.
- Separate sourced facts, inference, and open questions.
- Challenge claims and look for counter-evidence before concluding.
- Preserve citations and source URLs in durable reports.
- Use focused domain skills from this root's `.pi/skills/`, and make one global
  only when it applies to nearly every workflow.
